# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This is the entry point to OctoAcme's project management process documentation. Together, these guides describe a customer-first, iterative, data-informed approach to delivery, with clear ownership, stakeholder alignment, quality practices, proactive risk management, disciplined releases, and continuous improvement.

## Project Management Lifecycle

1. **Initiation:** Validate the problem and expected outcomes, identify stakeholders, define success measures and an initial timeline, and decide whether to proceed.
2. **Planning:** Turn approved work into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, milestones, a Definition of Done, test plans, risks, and dependencies.
3. **Execution and tracking:** Deliver in small increments, track work and progress on the project board, use reviewed pull requests and CI/testing, monitor outcomes, and escalate blockers.
4. **Risk management and communication:** Maintain and review a risk register, monitor dependencies, share regular status updates, and escalate issues to the appropriate level.
5. **Release and deployment:** Confirm acceptance criteria and CI/security checks, prepare release notes, smoke tests, and rollback plans, validate in staging, deploy, verify, and communicate the release.
6. **Retrospective and improvement:** After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, capture learnings and turn them into owned, trackable improvement actions.

## Key Roles

- **Project Manager:** Coordinates delivery, timelines, risks, and communication.
- **Product Manager:** Defines outcomes, prioritizes work, and measures success.
- **Developers:** Design, build, test, and deliver features.
- **QA/Testing:** Validates quality and acceptance criteria.
- **Stakeholders:** Provide input, alignment, and approvals.
